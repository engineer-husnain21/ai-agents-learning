ABOUT TAMAM LIVING
Tamam Living manages three residential buildings in Dubai: Marina
Heights (Dubai Marina), Deira Court (Deira), and Silicon Gardens
(Silicon Oasis). This handbook applies to all tenants in all three
buildings unless a building addendum has been approved in writing by
the Operations Director.

RENT AND PAYMENT
Rent is due on the 1st of each month. A grace period of 5 calendar
days applies. Payments received after the grace period incur a late
fee of AED 250. Rent is paid by bank transfer or through the tenant
portal; cash is not accepted at reception.

MAINTENANCE REQUESTS
Emergencies (water leak, electrical failure, no air conditioning, lift
entrapment) receive a response within 4 hours, 24 hours a day.
Routine requests (appliance faults, minor plumbing, fixtures) are
attended to within 3 working days. Requests are logged through the
tenant portal or at reception.

Tenants may not arrange their own contractors for works affecting
building systems. Any such works are charged back to the tenant.

PETS
Cats are permitted. Dogs require written approval from building
management before the animal enters the building. A maximum of one
pet per unit applies. Tenants are responsible for any damage or
disturbance caused by their pet.

PARKING
Each unit is allocated one parking bay. A second bay may be rented for
AED 300 per month, subject to availability in that building. Visitor
parking is limited to 4 hours and requires a pass from reception.

MOVING OUT
Tenants must give 90 days written notice before vacating. The unit is
inspected within 3 working days of handover. Security deposits are
returned within 21 days of a satisfactory inspection, less any
deductions, which are itemised in writing.

NOISE AND COMMON AREAS
Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Common areas, including the pool
and gym where provided, close at 10:00 PM. Barbecues on balconies are
not permitted in any building.
