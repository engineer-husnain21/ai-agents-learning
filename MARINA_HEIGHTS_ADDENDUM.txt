MARINA HEIGHTS — BUILDING ADDENDUM
Draft, circulated by building management, September 2026
Status: not yet approved by the Operations Director

This addendum sets out rules specific to Marina Heights. Where it
differs from the Tamam Living Tenant Handbook, tenants of Marina
Heights should follow this document.

MOVING OUT
Tenants of Marina Heights must give 60 days written notice before
vacating. Handover inspections take place on the day of key return.

PETS
No dogs are permitted in Marina Heights under any circumstances,
including registered assistance animals. Cats remain permitted, to a
maximum of one per unit.

PARKING
Second parking bays at Marina Heights are AED 400 per month due to
limited availability in the Marina area.

GYM AND POOL
The Marina Heights gym is open 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. The pool is
closed for maintenance every Tuesday morning until 12:00 noon.

DELIVERIES
Large deliveries must be booked with reception 24 hours in advance and
use the service lift only.
