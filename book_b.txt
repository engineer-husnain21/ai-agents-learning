The Voyage of the Red Comet

Captain Mira Solen commanded the research ship Red Comet on a five-year survey of the outer planets. Her crew of twelve scientists mapped the rings of Saturn and collected ice samples from Europa.

On day 411, the ship's botanist Dr. Kael Voss discovered a frozen moss inside a Europa ice core. When warmed in the lab, the moss released a pale blue gas that made the air smell like rain. Mira ordered the sample sealed.

Three weeks later, the moss began to glow. Kael named it Solen's Moss after the captain. It grew only in low gravity and died if exposed to Earth's air.

The mission's greatest danger was not the moss but a micrometeor storm near Jupiter. The Red Comet lost one solar panel. Engineer Priya Nair repaired the power grid in six hours using spare copper from the greenhouse frame.

When the ship finally turned home, Mira wrote in her log: "We did not find people among the stars. We found a plant that remembers water, and a crew that remembers why we left."
